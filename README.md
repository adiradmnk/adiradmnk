### 🌟 Featured Projects

#### 1. [Ilmora Whiteboard](https://ilmora.co.id)
> **Role:** Founder & CEO & Full Stack Developer @ PT ILMORA DIGITAL INNOVATION  
> **Tech:** Python, Go, Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Google Gemini API  
- Real-time AI-driven visual tutoring platform projecting AI voice explanations into an interactive canvas.
- Features synchronized visual engines, modular grid layouts, and precision smart inking to emulate professional 1-on-1 tutoring.

#### 2. MCSYS - Medical AI Claims & Receipt OCR
> **Role:** Artificial Engineer Intern @ PT MCSYS CARE SOLUTION  
> **Tech:** Python, Go, Next.js, TrOCR, PaddleOCR, Gemini API, Ollama, Docker, Linux  
- Developed an end-to-end OCR pipeline based on Vision-Language Transformer (TrOCR) for medical receipt data extraction.
- Built a developer platform with API key management and self-hosted local AI inference on internal servers ensuring data privacy and low latency.
- Normalized medical receipts to TPA 8-category structured JSON format.

#### 3. [ClinicThinking](https://clinicthinking.com)
> **Role:** Frontend & Artificial Intelligence Developer  
> **Tech:** Next.js, React Flow, Python (FastAPI), Gemini API, Go, PostgreSQL, TypeScript  
- Interactive clinical reasoning platform designed to train medical students and mitigate cognitive bias (*premature closure* & *anchoring bias*).
- Features real-time whiteboard reasoning maps and adaptive AI feedback based on illness script theory.

#### 4. EDLIG (Education Disability Learning Inclusive Game)
> **Role:** Project Leader  
> **Tech & Method:** Mobile & Kinect Game, Scrum Methodology  
- Educational game designed for deaf and hard-of-hearing students to improve basic mathematics comprehension and communication skills.
- Implemented and evaluated directly with teachers and students at **SKHN 01 Pandeglang**.
- 🥇 **Gold Medal** — World Invention Competition and Exhibition (WICE) 2024 (Innovative Social Science Category, Selangor, Malaysia)  
- 🥇 **Gold Medal** — Jakarta International Science Fair (JISF) 2024 (Social Science Category)

#### 5. BEFU (Better Future)

- Mobile application tracking vehicular carbon emissions during travel paired with environmental education modules.
- Adopted and tested by **100++ active users** across Indonesia.

#### things i've built

**[ilmora-whiteboard](https://ilmora.co.id)** — the tutor explains out loud while strokes appear on the canvas,
kept in sync with the speech. Hardest part so far: making it feel instant. Next.js, Go, Python, Gemini, Postgres.

**[lanjut.id](https://github.com/adiradmnk/lanjut.id)** — predicts which merchants in the BNI ecosystem are about to cancel,
asks them *why* with surveys Gemini writes on the fly, then offers a deal that doesn't lose money. Go (Gin) + a FastAPI sidecar.

**medical-claims-ocr** — fine-tuned TrOCR and PaddleOCR to read crumpled clinic receipts and sort every line
into the 8 categories insurers (TPA) expect. Self-hosted, because medical data shouldn't leave the building.

**[clinic-thinking](https://clinicthinking.com)** — a simulator for medical students. Your diagnosis is drawn as a graph,
and the AI taps you on the shoulder when you're anchoring on the first idea or closing the case too early.

**edlig-game** — math and sign-language games for deaf and hard-of-hearing kids, played with body movement
(phone camera or Kinect). Tested with students at SKHN 01 Pandeglang. Gold at WICE 2024 and JISF 2024.

**befu-climate** — tracks the carbon footprint of your commute. ~100 commuters used it;
the paper behind it won 1st place + best presenter at UISO 2024, Universitas Indonesia.
